        Help
        
        
        
          How to get your X cookies
          
          
            
        1. On your computer, open x.com in a normal browser and log in.
          2. 
            
        2. Press F12 to open the developer tools. On a Mac in Chrome, press Cmd + Option + I.
          3. 
            
        3. Click the Application tab. In Firefox it is called Storage. If you can't see it, click the » arrows to show more tabs.
          4. 
            
        4. On the left, open Cookies and click https://x.com.
          5. 
            
        5. Find the row named auth_token and copy its Value. Then do the same for the row named ct0.
          6. 
            
        6. Paste them into the two boxes in Settings above and press Save settings.
          7. 
          
        
          
        
            
        Treat these two values like a password. Anyone who has them can act as your X account, so never share them.
        
            
        If you log out of x.com in that browser, they stop working.
        
            
        If the bot later says "Not logged in", just repeat these steps to get fresh values.
